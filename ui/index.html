Which camera?

Search every Canon model CHDK supports, by any of its names (IXUS, ELPH, SD, IXY, PowerShot).

How should CHDK start?

This decides how the SD card has to be formatted in the next step.

Choose the SD card

Insert the card into your Mac. Only FAT-formatted removable cards are listed.

Format this card…

Erases everything on the card.

Firmware version

CHDK must exactly match the firmware in your camera.

How do I find my firmware version?

    The camera only shows the version when this empty file is on the card. Eject the card, check the version in the camera, then put the card back.

    Install CHDK

    Package

      Allow access to the card

      1. Click Open Full Disk Access.
      2. Click + under the list (or drag the app in from Finder), choose CHDK SD Creator, and turn it on.
      3. Click Restart app, then press Install CHDK again.

      CHDK is ready

      Start CHDK on your camera

              1. Click Open Settings.
